# Surface Brief: CPNS GO App (public/index.html)

**Scope & Visitor Mode**
- Scope: Main SPA Application
- Mode: Operate

**Audience, Job, Action, Proof, Constraints**
- Audience: CPNS candidates (Fresh grads, honorer, serial applicants).
- Job: Master exam logic (TWK, TIU, TKP) on the go.
- Action: Complete 5-question micro-quizzes, score ≥ 3 to progress.
- Proof/Content: Action-Point-Solution (APS) breakdowns for every question.
- Constraints: Single-file SPA, Google Apps Script backend, mobile-first design, strict progression lock.

## Direction contract
**THESIS**: Learning is not a casual game; it's a rigorous audit of knowledge where progression is strictly calculated. We refuse the standard casual quiz app look.
**OWN-WORLD**: Fintech glassmorphism. Light grey-blue base (#F3F4F8) with soft lavender/peach mesh gradients. High transparency white glass cards, strict black/grey text, and vibrant status colors (purple/green/red) for metrics.
**STORY**: The visitor completes strict micro-learning modules (audits). A score of ≥ 3 earns a definitive 'validated' stamp, unlocking the next module. Failure requires a re-audit.
**FIRST VIEWPORT**: Mobile-first composition. A prominent "Total Spending" style header showing the user's overall progress/score. Below it, a 4-column quick action grid, followed by a vertical stack of "Subscription Row" style module cards. The primary action (start next module) sits prominently in a floating bottom nav.
**FORM**: The Fintech Audit Ledger (Ground candidate 5, seed key ddd322bf). Raised with definitive validation stamps (from Jet-Age Ticket) and a strict visible armature (from Crouwel Grid).
**FINISH**: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
